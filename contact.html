FAQs

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is always here to help.

How do I open a K Bank account?

You can open a K Bank current account entirely online in under 10 minutes. You'll need a valid UK address, ID, and a few minutes to complete our identity checks.

Is my money safe with K Bank?

Yes. K Bank is authorised by the PRA and regulated by the FCA. Your eligible deposits are protected up to £85,000 per person under the FSCS.

How do I report a fraudulent transaction?

If you spot a transaction you don't recognise, freeze your card immediately in the app and contact our fraud team on 0800 999 5678 — available 24/7.

Can I transfer my account from another bank?

Absolutely. K Bank supports the Current Account Switch Service (CASS), which moves your direct debits and standing orders automatically within 7 working days.

What is the K Bank app available on?

The K Bank app is available for free on iOS and Android. It gives you full control of your accounts, cards, and transfers from your phone.

How long do international transfers take?

Most international transfers arrive within 1–3 business days. Transfers within the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA) typically arrive the next business day.
